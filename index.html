      Method. Games missed = team regular-season games (82 every season in this window) − player games played. Traded players: 82 − combined GP across all teams, team shown as e.g. BRK/PHI. Players on a team's salary sheet with 0 GP count as 82 missed. "$ lost" = salary × missed ÷ 82 (a cap-hit proration, not money actually forfeited). "$ / GP" = salary ÷ games played. In-Season Tournament final not counted (it's not a regular-season game). Missed games include injury, illness, rest, suspension, personal leave, two-way/G League assignments, and DNP-coach's decision — the data can't tell them apart.

      Sources. Stats: Basketball-Reference league per-game & advanced tables (per game, advanced, 2022–2026). Salaries: Basketball-Reference team pages, "Salaries" table (e.g. BOS 2025). Players joined by Basketball-Reference player ID. Data pulled Oct 30, 2026. Dataset: player_seasons.csv · data.json.
